## What the AGPL asks of you

This is a summary, not legal advice; the [LICENSE](LICENSE) text is what applies.

- **Keep the notices.** Keep the copyright notices and the licence, and give recipients a copy of the AGPL.
- **Share your source when you distribute.** If you give anyone a copy of robroz or of a modified version (binaries, containers, installers), you must also provide the complete corresponding source code under the AGPL-3.0.
- **Modified versions stay AGPL.** A work based on robroz, including your changes, must be licensed as a whole under the AGPL-3.0. You cannot add further restrictions.
- **Network use counts (section 13).** If you modify robroz and let users interact with it over a network, for example as a hosted service, SaaS or internal web tool used by others, you must offer those users the complete corresponding source code of your modified version, at no charge, through a standard means such as a download link. Not distributing binaries does not exempt you.

If you use robroz unmodified, or you are happy to publish all your changes under the AGPL-3.0, you do not need a commercial licence, even for commercial use.

## When you need a commercial licence

You need a commercial licence from the copyright holder if, for example, you want to:

- ship robroz, or a product that includes or is based on it, as closed-source or proprietary software;
- run a modified robroz as a service for others without publishing your modifications;
- combine robroz with code whose licence is incompatible with the AGPL-3.0;
- get terms the AGPL does not offer (warranty, indemnity, support).
